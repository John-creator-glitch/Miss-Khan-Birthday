Upload EVERYTHING in this folder to the ROOT of the repo Miss-Khan-Birthday (Settings > Pages > main / root).
IMPORTANT: nadab.mp3 (your voice note) must be uploaded too, next to index.html.
After uploading, check this opens/plays:  https://john-creator-glitch.github.io/Miss-Khan-Birthday/nadab.mp3
Then open the site once with ?v=4 at the end to skip old cache.

Film length is about 4.5 minutes (including the 31 second voice note).
If the voice note cannot load, the film now shows "VOICE NOTE COULD NOT LOAD" with a retry button instead of skipping silently.
Optional subtitles for the voice note: fill voiceCaptions in config.js, e.g. [[0,"Hi Miss Khan"],[5000,"..."]] (milliseconds from the start).
Tap the screen anytime to pause. The bottom row (I-V, heart) jumps between chapters.
